# Processing of Sensitive Personal Data

## Description

Processing of sensitive personal data (e.g., health-related diagnoses, biometric data) without a statutory basis in a formal law.

## Affected Projects

- Automated Correction of Handwritten Schoolwork

## Mitigation

Ensure a statutory basis in a formal law (e.g., cantonal elementary school laws) and conduct a DPIA to assess risks.
