# Data Protection Violations

## Description

Processing of personal data without a legal basis or in violation of data protection principles (e.g., lawfulness, proportionality, purpose limitation).

## Affected Projects

- Automated Correction of Handwritten Schoolwork

## Mitigation

Identify applicable legal bases, conduct a Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA), and ensure compliance with data protection principles (Privacy by Design, Privacy by Default).
