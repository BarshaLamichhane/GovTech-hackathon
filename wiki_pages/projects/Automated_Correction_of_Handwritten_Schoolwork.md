# Automated Correction of Handwritten Schoolwork

## Summary

School pupils used a smartphone scan to automatically correct their handwritten math work and spelling exercises using AI-based image recognition.

## Ai Use Case

Automated correction of handwritten learning content via AI-based image recognition

## Sector

Education

## Data Used

Handwritten worksheets, potentially including personal data (e.g., names, handwriting characteristics)

## Risks

- Data protection violations
- Unauthorized access to personal data
- Processing of sensitive personal data (e.g., health-related diagnoses)
- Copyright infringement from use of teaching materials
- Profiling of pupils
- Data security breaches

## Lessons

- Involve multiple stakeholders (school management, ICT officers, data protection authorities) early in the project
- Clarify legal bases and responsibilities under data protection law
- Implement Privacy by Design and Privacy by Default principles
- Conduct a Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA) for AI projects
- Avoid or minimize creation of personal data
- Ensure compliance with data protection principles (lawfulness, proportionality, purpose limitation, transparency)

## Regulations

- Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP)
- Cantonal data protection laws (e.g., Zurich's Information and Data Protection Act - IDG)
- Cantonal elementary school laws (Volksschulgesetze)
- Copyright Act (Urheberrechtsgesetz - UrG)
- Municipal and school-specific directives
