# Copyright Infringement

## Description

Use of copyrighted teaching materials or data for AI training without the author's consent or a legal exception.

## Affected Projects

- Automated Correction of Handwritten Schoolwork

## Mitigation

Obtain prior consent from authors for use of teaching materials or rely on legal exceptions (e.g., data mining for scientific purposes).
